About Rookwise

Rookwise is a free, independent tool that rates every move of your chess games – brilliant moves, blunders, accuracy, game rating and the best continuations. The analysis runs entirely in your browser.

Independence

Rookwise is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Chess.com or any other chess website. "Chess.com" is a trademark of its owner and is used here only to describe where your public games are imported from (the public Chess.com Published-Data API).
