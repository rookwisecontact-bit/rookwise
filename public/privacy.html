Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short

No account, no tracking cookies. Your analyses are stored only in your own browser.

What we process

  • Username you type. It is sent to our server only to download your public games from the Chess.com Published-Data API and is not stored by us.
  • Your analyses and settings (board colours, piece style, last username) are saved in your browser (IndexedDB / localStorage). You can delete them anytime by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may process standard request data such as IP address for security and performance.

Third parties

Game data comes from the public Chess.com Published-Data API. Hosting: Cloudflare. We do not sell personal data.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask about, correct or delete data related to you. Contact: rookwise.contact@gmail.com
